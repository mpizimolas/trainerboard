# TrainerBoard — DM Scripts for 3-Trainer Validation
Live URL: https://trainerboard.vercel.app

## 1) Cold DM (Instagram / Facebook)
> Hey [Name], saw your work with [specific post] — quick Q: how do you send workouts & track who actually did them? Sheets/WhatsApp?
>
> I built TrainerBoard (https://trainerboard.vercel.app) for independent trainers — create template once, assign in 1 click, client gets magic link (no app to install), you see Today's check-ins live. Looking for 3 trainers to try free for 2 weeks for feedback (no card). Worth a 5-min look? Your dashboard: https://trainerboard.vercel.app/dashboard — add a test client, then open its magic link.

Personalize 1 detail from profile. Send 15/day.

## 2) Follow-up (2 days later, if no reply)
> No worries if busy — 2 slots left for free feedback group. Happy to send 60-sec Loom instead? Example client view: https://trainerboard.vercel.app/c/tok_alex123

## 3) Facebook Group Post (value-first)
> For online coaches: what do you use to assign workouts? I tried TrueCoach/Trainerize but they’re $50+ for <20 clients. Built TrainerBoard (https://trainerboard.vercel.app) — free for 3 clients, $19 for 15. Magic link for clients, check-ins for you. Looking for 3 testers for free 2 weeks — anyone open to trying?

Post in: Personal Trainers Network, Online Fitness Coaches

## 4) Reply when they say Yes
> Awesome — here’s 60-sec Loom: [your Loom link]. To test: 1) https://trainerboard.vercel.app/dashboard → Add client (add Bio & Goals so I can tailor) 2) Create workout 3) Assign → Open client link on phone. Can I check in in 3 days to see how it went?

## 5) Closing Questions (after they try loop)
1. What do you use now & most annoying part?
2. If this saved 2 hrs/week, what would you pay? (listen before pitching $19)
3. What’s missing that would make you *need* this?
4. Would you do $19/mo for 15 clients after 2 free weeks? Yes/No/Maybe?
5. Can I follow up in 3 days after you try with a real client?

Log answers in 3-trainer-tracker.csv
